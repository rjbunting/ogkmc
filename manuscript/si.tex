\section{Reaction list}

The complete \ce{O2}-stabilized reaction lists used for the kinetic Monte Carlo simulations are listed below.
These are the reaction lists used for the quasi-equilibrium dissolution sampling described in the main text.
Each entry includes the reaction template, free energy change, barrier, and rate where applicable.
The unstable one-site adsorbates removed from the one-site reaction list are shown in Table~\ref{tab:unstable-one-site-adsorbates}.
The excluded two-site structures and transition-state templates are shown in Tables~\ref{tab:excluded-structures} and \ref{tab:excluded-ts}, respectively.
The complete printable reaction lists follow in Tables~\ref{tab:si-one-site-reaction-list}--\ref{tab:si-dissolution-5-reaction-list}.
